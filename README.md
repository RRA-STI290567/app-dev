# My Favorite Shows and Movies 🎬

## 1. Pacific Rim
I love robots and how they fight. The action scenes are intense, and the concept of giant mechas battling monsters is really exciting. I also enjoyed the plot and especially the twist in *Pacific Rim: Uprising* (the sequel).

## 2. Lupin the 3rd
This anime is one of the shows I grew up watching. It was one of the series that introduced me to anime and made me interested in watching more. The characters and adventures are fun and memorable.

## 3. Code Geass
One of my all-time favorite anime/shows. I really like how Lelouch, the protagonist, carries out his plans and executes his revenge. His intelligence and strategies make the story very engaging.
